replz: `replz scorecard` scores how well a repository's checks catch bugs from its history alone (escape rate by named and SZZ-traced fixes, provedone receipts, and recall and false alarms from the last recorded run), each with a `next:` fix, and `--ratchet` holds it to bands. provedone: a `provedone/receipt` entry point, so a caller reads a receipt's verdict without loading the CLI.
