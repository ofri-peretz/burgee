Container mode: a full disk now gives one line naming the fix. A sync or install that fails with the volume nearly out of room exits with its own code and says so; `docker create`, `cp`, `volume create`, the host's `tar` and the copy back each name `oxmutate prune`, `docker|podman system prune` or the Podman machine's disk size. Podman's short-name refusal tells you to use a full image name, and "unable to connect to Podman socket" is reported as a daemon that is not running.
