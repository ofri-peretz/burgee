New entry point `roundel/colorette`: colorette 2's forty-one styles as named exports, `isColorSupported` and `createColors({ useColor })`, graded 67 / 67 by colorette 2.0.20's own suite, level with colorette itself, and bundled at 2,438 B against colorette's 2,602. Colour is colorette's own rule, decided once at import — a `NO_COLOR` or `FORCE_COLOR` that is set at all, `--no-color` and `--color`, Windows, a terminal with a `TERM`, and `CI` on GitHub Actions, GitLab or CircleCI — not `roundel/policy`'s; with colour off every style is `String`, as colorette's are. `roundel/policy` does not change.
