`flagstaff/import` — `fromCliSpinners()` and `fromCliBoxes()` turn the corpora you already have into ordinary plugins, through the same `register()` and the same schema. Neither corpus is bundled; 838 B, reaching nothing. The plugin contract gains `borders`, and the built-in border styles move into the built-ins plugin with the spinners, so `box()` can draw with a style a plugin registered.
