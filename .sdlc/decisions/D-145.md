**A major is claimed only when that major's own suite grades the drop-in level with the incumbent (D-137); an older major that is not level is graded, published and not claimed.** `SUPPORTED_MAJORS` in `burgee/src/compat.ts` is the claim and `burgee migrate` reads it for its off-major check, so a claimed major is one `migrate` rewrites; `compat-oracle`'s `PREVIOUS_MAJORS` is the measurement — each older major's suite vendored at its last tag, its incumbent installed beside it for the control, a baseline under `baseline/majors/`, graded by the ratchet job's `--majors` steps — and `supported-majors-lock.test.ts` fails on a claimed major below its control. Today every range is the current major alone. Commander 14 misses two cases on one message change (15 names the surplus argument in `too many arguments`, 14 does not); yargs 17 misses 603, on one decision of 18's — 17's `require('yargs')` is a singleton, 18 removed it, and four of its suite files abandon their blocks in `beforeEach`. The alternative was to claim both with the gaps footnoted, which is the sentence the page used to carry ("programs written for 12–14 run unchanged wherever 15 kept their API") with a number beside it, and `migrate` would then rewrite a commander 14 program onto an error text it does not expect. Reaching level on either is a façade change for its lane: a major-aware message, or a CommonJS singleton — the second is a real surface, not a shim, and is not decided here
