**Four requirements, appended to `.sdlc/intents/controlroom/spec.md` as R15–R18; D-158 stands.** **R15, one render engine:** controlroom never paints on its own; every frame goes through flagstaff's repaint loop and each widget's static projection, and controlroom only lays out regions and routes keys, with a lock that fails on a stdout write under `packages/controlroom/src` outside the flagstaff path. **R16, the widget contract:** the flagstaff surface controlroom consumes is pinned by a lock on both sides. **R17, the Ink ecosystem unchanged:** `@inkjs/ui`, `ink-spinner`, `ink-text-input` and `ink-select-input` run unmodified with `'ink'` resolved to `controlroom/ink` by a documented alias or `overrides`, and `@inkjs/ui`'s own suite is graded through it in `compat-oracle` with a `--control` run, as R13 grades Ink's. This moves `@inkjs/ui` from the intent's Out of scope into v1, with no façade of its own. **R18, blessed, neo-blessed and terminal-kit:** no drop-in, because their surfaces are too large to reproduce honestly; a coming-from guide for each, `burgee migrate` codemod rules for the common screen, box, list and key patterns, and a docs section saying which package a reader needs, flagstaff or controlroom
