**A second `run()` returns the in-flight run's promise.** Every trigger's leave then waits for the same bounded shutdown: the deadline still ends it, `restore` is invoked first, and the process dies of the first signal because the first leave queued on that promise is the one that raises. After `runSync` there is nothing in flight and its report is returned as before. **Rejected: gating in `install.ts`.** The bug is in what `run()` promises, not in one caller. A shared flag in `install()` would fix the signal listeners and leave the crash listeners and every direct caller of `registry.run` with the same early resolve. `closeout/exit-hook` checks `runner.settled` and returns without leaving, so it is unaffected. **Weight:** burgee bundled 24,277 → 24,261 B, under the 24,282 ceiling, which is not moved. Measured on `registry.ts` minified alone, the fix costs 22 B and is paid for in that file: 16 B from dropping `state`'s explicit `finished: undefined`, and 23 B from building the async report in a local. `'beforeExit'` keeps its `unref`'d clock (D-164's first reason stands). Proven: two spawned cases in `terminal-restore.test.ts` (loop held, `flush` parked, SIGINT then SIGINT or SIGTERM) end at `raw-on, enter-alt, hide` on the unfixed build and pass on the fix, dying of SIGINT. `exit-hook` 21 / 21, and closeout passes 164 / 164
