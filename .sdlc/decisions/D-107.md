**The gate. Measured, and the fix costs nothing.** For weeks a rotating cast of suites failed inside `turbo run test` and passed alone — `bellpull`'s matrix and escape, `caique`'s conformance, the `exit-hook` compat row, `seniority`'s lilconfig and rc, `burgee`'s dev loop — and it was recorded as a property of those suites. It is not. **Every failure was `Error: Test timed out in 5000ms.` and every failing suite touches the filesystem or spawns a process**, and the battery's own concurrency is the variable: forced so nothing came from cache, `--concurrency=1` was 2 of 2 clean, `--concurrency=2` 2 of 2 clean at 82 s, `--concurrency=3` 1 of 2 failed, and the default of 10 failed **2 of 4** at 45 s. The clincher is a timing: `seniority`'s `rc()`, the call inside three of the failing cases, runs in **0.5 ms idle and 159 ms under load** — two orders of magnitude short of the 5,420 ms the cases were killed at. The test was never the problem and neither was `rc()`; vitest's 5 s default is a budget for a test body that *computes*, and these bodies wait on a disk while ten sibling tasks use it. Two fixes were measured and one was kept. **Bounding turbo to two tasks works and costs +37 s on every push, forever.** **Capping vitest's worker pool to 2 does not work** — 2 of 4 either way, because the contention is between tasks and not inside one; it was written, measured, and reverted rather than shipped with a confident comment. What ships is `vitest-timeouts.config.ts`: 30 s for a test and 60 s for a hook, shared by every package suite, chosen to sit far above "the machine is busy" and far below "forever" so a genuine hang still fails a build. Result: **8 of 8 clean**, four forced batteries at full concurrency and four `ci:local` runs, against a baseline of 2 in 4 failing
