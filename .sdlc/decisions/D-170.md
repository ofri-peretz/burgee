**Yes, both, released as 1.0.0 on the owner's decision of 2026-09-28; every other package stays 0.x.** They are the only two that meet every written 1.0 criterion. linegauge: its spec is 13 of 13 built, and every drop-in path passes its incumbent's own suite in full, graded at the newest release: string-width 8.3.0 233 / 233, wrap-ansi 10.0.2 85 / 85, strip-ansi 7.2.0 8 / 8, slice-ansi 9.0.1 104 / 104, each against a control of the same count. flagstaff: its spec is 12 of 12 built, and ora 9.4.1 99 / 99, log-update 8.0.0 99 / 99, boxen 8.0.1 84 / 84, cli-table3 0.6.5 29 / 29. **The regrade before the freeze found two linegauge defects, and both were fixed before 1.0.** (1) A ReDoS in `width()`: the zero-width test, a `+` over an alternation of six Unicode classes, backtracked exponentially on a run of `U+034F`, which is in two of the classes; 26 joiners took 2.4 s and 1,000 did not finish. string-width 8.3.0's suite never reached its summary (0 / 233). A code-point loop replaced it: 1,000 joiners in 0.6 ms, 3,000,000 in about 200 ms, and it is checked against both old regexes over 177,156 short strings. (2) `wrap()` ran NFC over escape sequences, so `ESC[31m` + `U+0301` composed into `ESC[31ḿ` and OSC payloads were rewritten; wrap-ansi 10.0.2 read 81 / 85 until only the text between sequences was normalized. The same freeze fixed a flagstaff wiring bug: the tty projection counted painted rows with `split('\n')`, so a frame wider than the terminal left stale rows; it counts with linegauge's `lineCount` now. **What 1.0 promises:** every published entry point, the plugin `schema.json` and the bin under semver, and each drop-in graded 100% by the incumbent's own suite at the major named above. **Out of scope:** boxen 9 — `flagstaff/boxen` is accepted as a boxen 8 drop-in, and a program on boxen 9 is left alone by `burgee migrate` (`SUPPORTED_MAJORS`); earlier majors of every incumbent; `linegauge/truncate` and `linegauge/widest`, which are linegauge's own API with no incumbent suite. Changesets `linegauge-1-0.md` and `flagstaff-1-0.md` are majors; `updateInternalDependencies: "patch"` bumps burgee and caique
