**Corrected against itself, in the direction that costs us.** #415 moved B4 from `--outfile` to `--splitting --outdir` and published the **entry chunk** as `bundled`. That is wrong, and the reason is a detail of how esbuild splits: a module reachable both statically from the entry *and* dynamically from some branch goes into a **shared chunk**, which the entry then loads with a plain `import` statement — at startup, in a separate file the entry-chunk number does not count. Measured on `burgee/commander`: entry chunk **51,257 B**, initial load **59,157 B**, the difference being a 7,681-byte chunk holding `schema`, `plugin`, `definition` and `manifest`. `bundled` is now the transitive closure over `import-statement` edges, `dynamic-import` edges still cost nothing until they run, and every published figure moved against us on the same commit that introduced them: core 19,540 → 44,287, `burgee ÷ cac` 1.869 → 4.237, `burgee ÷ commander` 1.311 → 1.514. `core-under-52kb-bundled` stayed met at the honest number. Found before #415 merged, which is the only reason this is a correction and not a retraction
