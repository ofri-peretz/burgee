**Owner — three claims have a measured floor above their own gate, and closing the gap means deleting the product, not optimising it.** Everything engineering can reach has now been taken (D-100, D-101) and the residual is structural. **`lighter-than-cac` (2.740):** cac is 10,457 bundled bytes of parser and help renderer. burgee's 28,637 is parser + `validate.ts` (coercion, choices, relations, Standard Schema) + `seniority` precedence + `closeout`'s registry and terminal restore + agent detection + package discovery. Stripping every remaining conditional branch — `seniority`'s explain half, `plugin.ts`'s `validate`, closeout's hang deadline and its report — measures a floor near **23,600 bytes, or 2.26×**. **`lighter-than-commander` (1.514):** our `commander/command.ts` is 33,487 bundled against commander's 27,226, and the front-end also carries `bellpull/cross-spawn` (5,060 B, and it cannot go lazy — `commander/sync.test.ts` holds `parse()` synchronous and ~23 `executableSubcommand` cases in commander's own suite mock the spawn inside it, so the compat claim of 1360 / 1360 pays for the bytes). Even at byte parity with commander's own command.js the ratio lands near **1.09**: the claim asks our commander to be smaller than commander while also carrying a manifest, a schema and an MCP server. **`cold-start-at-or-below-cac` (1.683):** `import 'cac'` is one file in 4.2 ms; `import 'burgee'` is 17 in 17.4 ms, and the remaining five that could go lazy would leave ~12 — a ratio near **1.5**, not 1. Three options, and the default is the third: **(a)** delete features until the numbers fit, **(b)** restate the two cac rows against what a consumer actually has to install to reach parity — cac plus a config resolver plus a shutdown binder — which is the comparison U5's own words describe and the shape `roundel/tokens` already uses for picocolors (its entry says picocolors is "the *weight bar* the bet names, not a package roundel replaces"; cac was entered in `PAIRS` as a drop-in incumbent and there is no `burgee/cac`, no cac compat row), **(c)** leave all three on the README as **not met** with the measured floor beside them. Default while unanswered: **(c)** — a claim we cannot meet stays on the page saying so
