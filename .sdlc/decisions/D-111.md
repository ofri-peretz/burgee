**No. Every production dependency is in-family.** The owner, 2026-09-23: *"All our packages dependencies should be 'in-family'"*, *"we should avoid any external dependencies"*. A published package may depend on its siblings in this repository and on nothing else, and that covers all three fields npm installs from — `dependencies`, `peerDependencies`, `optionalDependencies`. Z3's and K1's *"zero runtime dependencies"* is restated to this, because it is what the family has always been: burgee, caique and flagstaff depend on the layers below them by design. `scripts/package-shape-lock.test.ts` held `dependencies` only; it now holds all three, proved by giving roundel an external `chalk` peer, which turned exactly that case red. devDependencies — test tooling, benchmark incumbents — are not shipped and are outside the rule; so is `compat-oracle`, which is private and whose peers are the incumbents it grades
