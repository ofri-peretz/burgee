**ansi-regex 6.6.0 (2026-11-08) is what the eleven 2026-11-08 issues were.** boxen, cli-truncate, ink, log-update, ora, slice-ansi 10.0.0, string-width, strip-ansi, widest-line, wrap-ansi and yargs each read as a `none` release that grew by 891 B. 6.6.0 rewrites the grammar:

- the CSI is ECMA-48's (any final from `@` to `~`);
- DCS, SOS, PM and APC end only on ST;
- the digit-final escapes (`ESC 7`, `ESC ( 0`) and the Linux palette are new;
- a CAN or SUB cancels an OSC.
