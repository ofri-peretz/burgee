**`whichSync` stops at the first `PATH` entry that answers, and an absent candidate is no longer an exception; with that, bellpull spawns at the raw `child_process` floor on the Linux runner and R8's spawn half is met there, 0.992–0.997 against a gate of 1.0 — with a margin under 1 %, which is recorded here rather than hidden.** D-20260930-bellpull-spawn-vs-tinyexec gated the pair at 1.0 on a Mac reading of 0.933, and #756 auto-merged before its own non-required bench job reported: **1.06 on ubuntu-latest**, 4.235 ms a call against tinyexec's 3.999 — red on main. It was not noise. A diagnostic run on the runner (branch `diag/bellpull-linux-spawn`, `bench.yml` dispatched, discarded after) timed each piece: `resolveExecutable('node')` alone cost **~195 µs a call** over the runner's 18 `PATH` entries, because `whichSync` was `whichAllSync()[0]` — it statted every entry after the one that answered — and each absent candidate was a throwing `statSync`, an Error with a stack. With `PATH` cut to node's directory bellpull read 0.995–0.998; tinyexec does no resolution at all on POSIX. With the spawn stubbed out, bellpull's JS costs ~10 µs over a bare spawn and tinyexec's ~210 µs on a Mac, so the rest of the library was never the problem. **The fix** (`which.ts`, `executable.ts`): one walk behind both answers with a `first` flag; `statSync(file, { throwIfNoEntry: false })`, keeping the catch for `ELOOP`/`EACCES`. Resolution on the runner: **~195 → ~15 µs**; the pair: **0.992, 0.997, 0.995** over three runs, where a raw `child_process.spawn` reads 0.977, 0.980, 0.994 — bellpull is at the floor, and in one run under it. `whichAllSync` still walks everything. **The margin.** Both libraries spend 3.6 ms a call waiting for `node --version`, so on Linux the ratio can go no lower than a raw spawn's ~0.98. A cheaper child (`true`) reads 0.970–0.988 with a wider per-round spread, and `true` does not exist on the Windows compat cells that run the parity check, so the child stays. The ceiling stays **1.0**: it is R8's bar, and the ratchet lock refuses a raise on the day the ceiling was set (`scripts/ratchet-steps.ts` compares a dated decision by its date). What was changed instead is the noise: 40 spawns a side a round, not 20. **If this pair reds on a runner while bellpull is still at the raw-spawn floor, that is the gate's headroom, not bellpull — a newer decision may give it a ceiling above 1.0 with the target left at 1.0, the shape every other B5 pair has; that is the owner's call and is not taken here.** Also in the fix PR: burgee/commander bundles the resolver, +65 B (60,848 → 60,913, 1.557× → 1.559×, under 61,100 and 1.565) and the generated figures were regenerated; B4's `bellpull ÷ tinyexec` bytes went **down**, 0.989 → 0.986; bellpull's tarball band 112,082 → 112,352 B.
