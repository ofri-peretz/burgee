**Pinned to the default it had, and the gap fixed upstream.** `eslint-plugin-conventions` 6.0.0 flipped this rule's default `preferred` from `in` to `Object.hasOwn`; diffed against 5.3.5 that single field is the whole behaviour change, the rule body is otherwise identical. The new default is wrong for TypeScript — `in` is the narrowing operator and `Object.hasOwn` is not, verified with `tsc --noEmit --strict`: reading a union member behind a `hasOwn` guard fails with TS2339, "Property 'reason' does not exist on type", naming the whole union rather than the member that has it, where the `in` guard compiles — and all 48 sites here are discriminated unions. Pinned `{ preferred: 'in' }` in `eslint.config.mjs` rather than turned off, so the consistency check still runs and the five deliberate `Object.hasOwn` uses in seniority keep their own disables. Nothing was silently rewritten: the rule refuses an autofix across the prototype boundary. The rule's page said none of this and now does — `ofri-peretz/eslint#1083`, merged
