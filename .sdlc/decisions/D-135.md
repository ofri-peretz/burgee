**Yes, through one seam — `seniority/src/runtime.ts` (Y9) — and only as a default.** `seniority/dotenv`'s `config()` populates the process environment and reads `./.env` when given neither, and `seniority/rc` reads the ambient environment when given none, because their incumbents do and their own suites assert it (`process.env.BASIC` after a bare `config()` in 34 cases; rc's `process.env[name + '_envOption']`). A drop-in that makes every migrating caller add an argument is not a drop-in. The resolver — `precedence`, `config`, `explain` — never imports the seam, and every façade still takes the world as an argument first. Measured: `dotenv` 80 → 106 / 141, `rc` 0 → 1 / 1.
