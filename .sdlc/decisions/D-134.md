**Raised to 59,600, the next fifty above the 59,586 measured (59,415 before), and the ratio ceiling to 1.53.** `_optionSpecs` is the projection every surface reads, and it has to be in the startup graph because `--schema` answers synchronously inside `parse()`. It now publishes a lone `--no-x` as `noX`, folds a `--x`/`--no-x` pair into one key, and marks which booleans commander negates (`OptionSpec.negatable`). Without it, `tools/call` sent flags commander refused and completions offered `--no-version`, `--no-skip-blank` and `--color`. **Not moved into a lazy module**: the projection is read by `--schema`, which cannot await. A first version cost 313 bytes with two `Set`s; one `some()` per option brought it to 171
