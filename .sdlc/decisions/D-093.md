**Owner — and the measurement says it is smaller than it looked.** Interleaved A/B, 21 alternating spawns per arm, medians: dropping `help`, `mcp` and `schema` from the import graph gives **1.33x and 1.44x** on two runs, about a quarter of `import 'burgee'`. But burgee's cold-start overhead above bare node is ~39 ms, so the ~6 ms saved moves `burgee ÷ cac` from 2.03 to roughly 1.94. That is a major version bump, three new subpaths (`burgee/help`, `burgee/mcp`, `burgee/schema`) and a migration note, for 5% of a ratio that has to halve. Default: **not now** — worth doing when something else brings the ratio close, not on its own. Recorded because an earlier report of this put it at -27.7 ms in raw milliseconds and made it look like the single big lever; interleaved, it is not.
