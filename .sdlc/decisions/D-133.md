**No — R6 restated to the subpaths.** An override points an incumbent's name at the package root, and a root has one default: `exit-hook` and `restore-cursor` each want it, and `signal-exit`'s `onExit` would collide with closeout's own. The drop-ins are `closeout/signal-exit`, `closeout/exit-hook` and `closeout/restore-cursor`, swapped by import; the README's `overrides` recipe for two incumbents at once never linked and is withdrawn. `scripts/override-recipe-lock.test.ts` fails any printed recipe whose root lacks the incumbent's exports.
